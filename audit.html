Three causes, measured

1. Walls traced from a picture, not the lines

The wall shapes were traced from a rendered image of the PDF at 8.8 mm per pixel, then simplified with a 2-pixel tolerance. Any face can sit up to ~18 mm off and lean slightly. A 15 mm "stroke" correction was then taken off each side, but about 19 mm was needed, so walls are 6–9 mm too thick and rooms 3–12 mm too small. Median wall-thickness error against the standard sizes: 8.0 mm.

2. Drawing scale off by 0.031 %

The pipeline uses 52.92 mm per PDF point. Grid axes 4→10 measure 756.092 pt for 40 000 mm on both p10 and p11, which gives 52.9036. Walls were placed with the first number, windows from the dimension chains in true millimetres. Measured on 37 window jambs, the windows sit 6 mm (north end) to 17 mm (south end) from their drawn jambs, a steady 0.25 mm per metre with only 1.2 mm scatter.

3. Hollow columns

Hatched columns and casings traced as a 35 mm outline ring around an empty middle. They look solid in renders (they are capped top and bottom), but the geometry is hollow.

The fix, tested on the copy

tools/snap_walls.py moves every traced wall face onto the PDF's own vector wall line (Revit draws each cut wall as a 0.43–0.51 pt outline centred on the real face). It fills the hollow columns, and restores anything the snap would lose. Scored the same way as the current model:

How it was done, and why this way

  1. The model was duplicated first (D:\NOMT_audit_copy_2026-10-10, MD5-checked). Everything was measured on the copy.
  2. Ground truth = the architect's printed dimensions, not the model's own data: 363 dimension strings and their tick marks read from the vector PDF. The drawing was checked against itself first.
  3. Each scored dimension was measured again in the model by cutting the walls at 1.2 m, in the building file and in each apartment FBX. Result: deviation per dimension, percentage within tolerance, and a colour deviation map.
  4. Graded against the USIBD Level of Accuracy bands (LOA30 = 5–15 mm at 95 % confidence). The worst flags were checked by eye on the drawing.
