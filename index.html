To confirm with the architect

found by the checks
  • Ц-2 height. The p19 table says 1500; Ц-2's own detail (20 + 1350 + 30) and elevation p18 say 1400. Modelled 1400. p19 · p18
  • “Ц-3” means two things. p18 tags the living-room window vents Ц-3; p19's Ц-3 is the balcony door-window. Each drawing's geometry was followed; the living-room window is not in the schedule. p18 · p19
  • Flat G balcony opening. The p11 dimension string gives 1980 + 1220; the DWG and the p19 schedule give 2000, like the other seven. Modelled 2000. p11 · DWG · p19
  • Balcony glazing Ц-5 / Ц-6 is tagged on the plans and p33 but missing from the p19 schedule. p11 · p33 · p19
  • Bronze colour. CMYK 37/52/19/0 on the sheets converts to mauve, while the drawing fill reads bronze-beige. Rendered as bronze. p16–p18 legend
  • "Open book" outline. Plans p5–p7 draw a second, thin outline 0.5 m outside the screen's thick outline; the east elevation p17 and the 1250 mm dimension match the thick one. Modelled on the thick outline. p5–p7 · p17
  • Choices made for the visualisation (not in the drawings): bedding, lamps and cushions (the beds and nightstands themselves are from the DWG); WC doors shown closed; 11 Revit furniture pieces (10 wardrobes, 1 kitchen unit) moved 4–10 cm out of walls they overlapped; balcony frame colour, surrounding blocks and trees are placeholders; the LED screen shows a placeholder "НОМТ ТӨВ". logged in scene_spec.json

How it was built

tools/
  1. Plan chains, door cut-outs and tags on p10/p11 give every opening; elevations p16–p18 give frames, sills and heads (extract_openings.py).
  2. The Revit DWG gives each door and window as a named element; a fitted transform compares both sources (extract_dxf.py, verify_sources.py).
  3. Walls, windows, doors, balconies, fins and the architect's furniture become a checked scene spec (scene_spec.py, furniture_spec.py, massing_spec.py).
  4. Blender builds and renders it; orthographic renders are overlaid on the sheets (blender_build.py, overlay_check.py).
  5. Camera paths are planned and checked through free space; frames render in EEVEE and are cut with Mongolian titles (shots.py, render_shots.py, edit_video.py).

The film itself is the Blender render. Higgsfield was used only to upscale it to 4K (Topaz) — the frames still come from the model, so windows and doors cannot drift. A photoreal AI restyle of the approach was tested and kept out of the film: trees popped in and the surrounding city was invented.
